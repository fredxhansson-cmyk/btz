- `ep-c3/c4/c5.wav` — TX81Z "FM Piano" (Rhodes/electric-piano), multisampled C3/C4/C5.
- `piano-c3/c4/c5.wav` — Grand Piano, Steinway B, multisampled C3/C4/C5.

Both trimmed, mono-summed, normalized and re-encoded to 16-bit for size.
Root note = C4 (MIDI 60) = the sampler's natural pitch (key 60).
- `organ-c3/c4/c5.wav` — Pipe Organ (VCSL, CC0), multisampled C3/C4/C5.
- `harpsi-c3/c4/c5.wav` — Harpsichord, French (VCSL, CC0), multisampled C3/C4/C5.
