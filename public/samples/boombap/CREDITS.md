# Boombap kit — sample credits

These one-shots are bundled with Fuse as a factory drum kit.

- **Source:** [Boochi44/free-drum-samples](https://github.com/Boochi44/free-drum-samples)
- **License:** Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) — public domain. Free to use,
  modify and redistribute, including commercially, with no attribution required.
- **Provenance:** derived from the TR-808 samples released as CC0 by Edward Loveall.

Files: `kick.wav`, `snare.wav`, `hat.wav`, `openhat.wav`, `clap.wav` (Soulful
Vintage kit), `808.wav` (Bounce kit, tuned to A1).

CC0 is chosen deliberately so the samples can ship inside the app with no
attribution or licensing burden. Before scaling the library, re-verify the
provenance of any additional packs — prefer CC0, and keep CC-BY packs in a
separately-credited tier.
